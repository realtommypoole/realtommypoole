## 🧭 How I approach problems

Define the problem → establish a baseline → evaluate alternatives → document trade-offs and limitations.

I’m interested in the space between “the demo works” and “this is worth deploying.”

That means asking:
- Does it solve the right problem?
- How are we measuring quality?
- What are the cost, risk, and operational trade-offs?
- What evidence would change our decision?
